WHERE WE'RE COMING FROM

Soft Hours is built around a single idea: what you wear when you enter and exit the day should be treated with care, intention, and patience. These are not pieces for sleep alone; they are part of how you enter and leave the day. The first hour and the last. They change the feeling of the hour, they make the beginning and end of your day more consicous.

For Chosen Solitude

TIME WELL KEPT

OUR FOUNDER

Soft Hours started with a frustration that we just couldn't shake. Our founder, Marion Voldan, had spent several years in the apparel industry surrounded by beautiful cloth made into clothes for going out. Pieces meant for home were always an afterthought: thin, disposable, made down to a cheap price that refelcted the lack of quality. The hours at the edges of the day are some of our most honest, so she set out to make the thing that didn't exist, working with a mill she trusted by hand and a workshop willing to finish slowly.

MADE WITH PATIENCE

A piece can only feel considered if it's made that way. From the mill that supplies our cloth to the atelier that cuts and finishes it, every part of Soft Hours is chosen for care over speed — including the studio that shaped the brand itself.

Fabric Supplier · Valle Mosso, Biella, Italy

ORMEZZANO FABRIC MILL

Our fabrics come from Ormezzano, a family-owned fabric mill based in the Biella Alps of Northern Italy. Materials this honest don't need embellishment.

Atelier · Lisbon, Portugal

ATELIER ESTORNINHO

Every garment is cut and finished by a small atelier in Portugal, Atelier Estorninho, where the work is intentionally slow and methodical.

Identity Designer · Denver, Colorado
