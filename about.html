WHERE WE'RE COMING FROM

Soft Hours is built around a single idea: what you wear when you enter and exit the day should be treated with care, intention, and patience. These are not pieces for sleep alone; they are part of how you enter and leave the day. The first hour and the last. They change the feeling of the hour and make the beginning and end of your day more conscious.

OUR FOUNDER

Soft Hours started with a frustration that we just couldn't shake. Our founder, Marion Voldan, had spent several years in the apparel industry surrounded by beautiful cloth made into clothes for going out. Pieces meant for home were always an afterthought: thin, disposable, made down to a cheap price that reflected the lack of quality. The hours at the edges of the day are some of our most honest, so she set out to make the thing that did not exist: clothing for the private hours, approached with the same intention as everything worn beyond the front door.
