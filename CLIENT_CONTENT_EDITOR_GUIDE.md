When you open Shopify, confirm that the theme name is **Soft Hours Ecommerce - Client Editor** before making changes.

## Seven steps for a safe update

1. Open **Edit content in Shopify** above.
2. Confirm that you are in **Soft Hours Ecommerce - Client Editor**.
3. Choose the page you want to update from the selector at the top.
4. Choose the relevant section in the left sidebar.
5. Update the copy or select new photography.
6. Check both the desktop and mobile previews.
7. Select **Save**, then use the ecommerce preview link to review the result.

## Where your edits live

### Theme editor

Use **Customize** for the Homepage, About page, collection presentation, product-page headings, Journal presentation, Size Guide, FAQ, Contact, navigation, footer, and newsletter.

### Products

Use **Products** for product names, descriptions, photography, prices, colors, sizes, inventory, and the supporting product fields: short description, details, fit notes, measurements, model information, fabric, care, maker story, dispatch estimate, delivery and returns summary, and related products.

### Content and policies

Use **Content** for Journal posts and menus. Use **Settings > Policies** for approved privacy, terms, shipping, and returns language.

If a product fact or customer promise is not final yet, leave that field blank. Blank fields will not appear as confirmed information on the storefront.
